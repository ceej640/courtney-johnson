Profile

Optical physicist and instrument builder. I design and build fluorescence microscopes from the optical table up, then pair them with computational methods — adaptive optics, high-speed 3D particle tracking, and volumetric point-scan imaging — so biologists can ask questions existing microscopes can't answer. I build toward microscopy methods capable of intelligent decision-making and systems-level biology.
